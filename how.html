🚚 Shipping & timing

Sea freight from Guangzhou to Douala then inland delivery typically takes 4–7 weeks. Small high-value items can go by air (1–2 weeks, quoted separately). Consolidation fee is 12% of goods value, minimum $45 — always shown before you confirm.

💳 Payment options

Mobile Money (MTN / Orange) in FCFA on delivery, bank transfer in USD before shipping, or cash on delivery in Douala. Every payment is confirmed on WhatsApp with a receipt.

Frequently asked questions

What does MOQ mean?

MOQ = Minimum Order Quantity — the smallest number of pieces a factory accepts per product. Prices drop as your quantity grows (see the tier table on each product).

Are these really factory prices?

Yes. We buy directly from vetted factories in Guangzhou wholesale markets, so there is no middleman markup. The tier table shows exactly how the unit price falls as quantity rises.

Can I mix different products in one shipment?

Absolutely — that is exactly what our consolidation service is for. Put whatever you need in the basket; everything ships together in one consignment for one shipping fee.

How long does delivery take?

Sea freight Guangzhou → Douala usually takes 4–7 weeks door-to-door depending on volume and customs. Air freight for small urgent parcels takes 1–2 weeks. We confirm the exact timeline with every order on WhatsApp.

What if something arrives damaged?

Every item passes QC inspection and we photograph your actual goods before packing. If something is still wrong on arrival, message us on WhatsApp with your order reference (TM-XXXXXX) within 48 hours and we will make it right.

I don\u2019t see what I need in the catalogue. Can you source it?

Yes! The catalogue is only a sample of 6,000+ factories. Send us a photo, video or description on WhatsApp and we will find it, quote you in USD and FCFA, and add it to your next shipment.
